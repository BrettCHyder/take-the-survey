The survey.

We do not ask for a name, email address, account, or login to vote. Our database stores aggregate Yes and No counts, Stripe payment reference IDs to prevent duplicate counts, and hashes of random browser identifiers for temporary voting limits. It does not store card numbers, security codes, payment email addresses, or raw IP addresses.

Cookies and spam protection.

A necessary, signed browser cookie lasts up to two hours and is refreshed while you use the survey. It supports a one-hour voting limit. A short-lived form token and automated request limits help stop spam. Clearing cookies or changing browsers may bypass the browser limit.

Browser cooldown records may remain after they expire until maintenance removes them. For new successful payments, we remove the browser hash from the payment record after recording the cooldown. Payment references remain for duplicate protection and reconciliation. Test data is kept separately from live responses.

Stripe and hosting providers.

Stripe collects the information needed to process your payment on its checkout page, which may include your email, name, billing details, and payment information. Stripe may offer its own optional features such as Link. Its records and policies are separate from our survey database. Our hosting and database providers, Vercel and Neon, may retain operational logs. Vercel processes network information, including IP addresses, to deliver the site and enforce request limits.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell survey data. Aggregate results are public. If you email support, your email provider and ours process that correspondence; avoid sending sensitive card information.
